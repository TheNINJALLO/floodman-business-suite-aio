Complete field guide

From the first call to the crew work order.

RoomFlow keeps the customer, drawing, room-by-room work, pricing, proposal, invoice, and crew instructions in one job. The safest workflow is linear: complete each guided step, review the shared scope once, and then generate every document from that same scope.

EstimatorCreate the job, draw rooms, assign work, review cost, and prepare documents.
OfficeOpen the saved job, update customer scope, issue proposal/invoice, and monitor status.
CrewUse the work order for selected rooms, wall-specific work, dimensions, equipment, and notes.

Fastest correct workflow

  1. Sign in and select the correct company.
  2. Choose Start New Estimate, or open an inbound email lead.
  3. Complete Guided Steps 1–6 in order. Use CAD Blueprint when the layout needs direct drawing.
  4. Review materials and private cost in Step 7.
  5. Open Documents, verify the shared scope, and choose which rows belong on the customer documents and work order.
  6. Generate the proposal, invoice, and crew work order. Save/export the job before leaving the site.
One source of truth: change quantities, customer prices, and document inclusion in the shared Documents scope. Proposal, invoice, and work order then use the same approved information.

2. Sign in and select a company

Your company controls which cloud jobs, permissions, equipment, and inbound leads you can see.

  1. Open RoomFlow and choose Sign In. New users can choose Sign Up when account creation is enabled.
  2. Enter your email and password. Do not share accounts; deletion and document actions are tied to permissions.
  3. Open More and confirm the Active workspace/company. Switch it before opening or creating a job if you work in more than one company.
  4. If your company is missing, use the company creation/join options available to your account or ask a company administrator.
  5. Use Log Out on shared devices when work is finished.
Wrong company? Stop before entering information. A job saved under one company will not appear when another company is selected.

The bottom navigation is designed for phones. The top controls expose drawing, file, document, and diagnostic tools.

Top controls

  • Guided Wizard / CAD Blueprint: switch between step-by-step entry and the full drawing workspace without creating a second job.
  • Undo / Redo: reverse or restore recent drawing changes.
  • New Project: clear the current working project and begin a new one. Save first.
  • Save Project / Load Project: export or import a RoomFlow job JSON file.
  • Job Database: open saved jobs.
  • Diagnostics: check browser/device capability and integration status.
  • Documents, Work Order, Proposal, Invoice: review or generate the project paperwork.
  • Level: set the active floor—crawlspace, basement, main, second, or attic—before adding rooms.

4. Start or open a job

Start a new estimate

  1. Open Jobs and tap Start New Estimate.
  2. Confirm that the new project is blank and that the correct company is active.
  3. Begin with Guided Step 1. A customer name and address make the job easier to identify later.

Continue an existing job

  1. Sign in, select the same company used on the other device, and open Jobs.
  2. Choose Refresh Shared to upload pending edits from this device and download the newest company jobs and saved estimates.
  3. Search by customer, address, or job name. A Cloud Shared badge identifies records available to other signed-in company devices.
  4. Choose Continue Estimate on the correct card.
  5. Confirm the customer name and latest room count before editing.
Cross-device rule: users do not need the Townsquare browser extension just to share RoomFlow jobs. They do need a RoomFlow sign-in, the same active company, internet access, and permission to view the job. Only estimate drafts saved to the cloud are available on another device.

Open a lead

  1. In the Leads panel, tap Refresh if the newest inquiry is not visible.
  2. Review every available field: name, phone, email, complete service address, appointment, estimator, source, email details, and issue.
  3. Tap Open in RoomFlow. The parsed contact and issue fields become the starting job information.
  4. For a fake or spam inquiry, tap Remove Lead and type REMOVE. This requires the delete_jobs permission and permanently removes the shared lead job.
  5. Correct any field that the email did not state clearly, then continue through the guide.
Email intake should assist data entry, not replace review. Confirm phone, service address, appointment, and issue with the caller.

5. Complete the nine Guided Wizard steps

Use Next only after reviewing the current step. You can return to an earlier step without losing the drawing.

Step 1 — Job Information

  1. Enter customer name, address, city, state, postal code, phone, and email.
  2. Set the estimator and appointment/date information.
  3. Add notes that belong to the overall project, not only one room.
  4. Add property or issue photos when available.
  5. Check spelling because these fields appear on customer documents.

Step 2 — Work Areas and Floors

  1. Select every level being inspected: crawlspace, basement, main, second, attic, or multiple areas.
  2. Set the active level before drawing its rooms.
  3. For multi-level work, finish one level at a time and switch levels from the top selector.

Step 3 — Rooms and Layout

  1. Add the first room as the anchor room. Enter measured width and length or draw a custom outline.
  2. Add connected rooms and place them against the correct shared wall.
  3. Add doors, windows, crawl doors, and hatches in their real locations.
  4. Open the 2D plan and check that rooms do not overlap or float away from the building.
  5. Use 3D as a review view; keep precise edits in 2D/CAD.

Step 4 — Work Needed

  1. Select a room, then enable only the work performed in that room.
  2. Set vapor barrier, drainage, wall, structural, cleaning, demolition, or insulation choices as applicable.
  3. For drywall removal, enter cut height and select the exact walls. Do not use a whole-room quantity when only one wall is cut.
  4. Add carbon straps or other wall work with automatic spacing or a verified custom quantity.
  5. Repeat for every room. Never assume a setting automatically applies to adjacent rooms.

Step 5 — Equipment and Structural Items

  1. Add sump systems, pumps, dehumidifiers, discharge paths, beams, posts, or stanchions.
  2. Place each item in the correct room on the blueprint.
  3. Open its details and enter model, capacity, quantity, or notes when required.
  4. Confirm access and discharge routes for the crew.

Step 6 — Measurement Review

  1. Compare every room dimension with field measurements.
  2. Review perimeter, floor area, wall quantities, openings, and equipment.
  3. Resolve overlap, detached-room, missing-dimension, or missing-scope warnings.
  4. Return to the affected room, correct it, and review again.

Step 7 — Materials and Internal Costing

  1. Review calculated materials and quantities.
  2. Choose margin or markup mode according to company policy.
  3. Set target margin, labor, rentals, custom items, and tax.
  4. Keep this screen private when a customer is viewing the device.

Step 8 — Project Documents

  1. Open the shared document scope.
  2. Review each description, quantity, customer price, and document checkbox.
  3. Add custom scope rows only when the calculated scope does not cover the work.
  4. Generate and inspect proposal, invoice, and work order before sending.

Step 9 — Save and Finish

  1. Save the job and wait for the save confirmation.
  2. Export a job JSON backup when the project is important or will be moved to another device.
  3. Print or save the required PDF documents.
  4. Return to Jobs and confirm the job card shows the latest customer and status.

6. Draw rooms, including angled and odd shapes

Rectangular room

  1. Select the correct level.
  2. Choose a room type such as living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, hallway, closet, staircase, or custom.
  3. On desktop, drag the room tool onto the canvas. On a phone, tap the tool and then tap where the room should be placed.
  4. Select the room and enter its measured dimensions in Details.
  5. Drag the room to connect it to the building. Zoom in to align shared walls.

Angled or non-rectangular room

  1. Choose Draw Angled Room.
  2. Tap or click the actual starting location on the blueprint. The first point is placed where you choose, not forced to the center.
  3. Add one point at each real corner, moving around the room in order.
  4. Finish/close the outline using the on-screen instruction. Avoid crossing one wall line over another.
  5. Select the completed room and drag individual corner handles to match field measurements.
  6. Zoom out and confirm the shape is attached to the correct neighboring wall and remains inside the intended floor plan.
Best practice: place the first two points on the wall that connects to the existing building. That makes the unusual room easier to position and adjust.

Fix a misplaced room

  1. Use Undo immediately if the shape was just created.
  2. Otherwise select the room, then drag the room body to move it or a corner handle to reshape it.
  3. Use zoom and grid alignment for small corrections.
  4. Delete and redraw only if the point order is wrong or walls cross.

7. Use the CAD Blueprint workspace

CAD Blueprint is the direct-edit workspace for complex layouts and precise equipment placement.

OpeningsDoor, window, exterior crawl door, hatchPlace on the intended wall, then use Details to verify type and measurements.

EquipmentSump, dehumidifier, discharge, interior pipe, stanchion, beamPlace in the real room and drag to the planned installation point.

Select, move, and edit

  1. Tap/click an item on the canvas. Its settings open in Details.
  2. Drag the item to move it. For polygon rooms, drag a corner handle to reshape it.
  3. Use Details for exact dimensions, quantities, room scope, wall work, and item notes.
  4. Use the delete control in Details only after confirming the correct object is selected.
  5. Use Undo if the result is wrong; use Redo if you reversed a correct edit.

Blueprint review

  • Rooms are on the correct level.
  • Shared walls touch without large gaps or overlaps.
  • Doors and windows are on the correct wall.
  • Equipment and discharge paths are visible where crews expect them.
  • Wall-specific work highlights only the intended walls.

8. Record work by room and by wall

Wall-specific selection is what turns a general estimate into useful crew instructions.

Drywall cutting

  1. Select the room receiving drywall work.
  2. Enable drywall removal/cutting and enter the cut height.
  3. Choose the wall-selection control, then tap each affected wall on the blueprint.
  4. Confirm the selected walls have the active visual highlight. Tap a wrong wall again to remove it.
  5. Review the calculated wall length/area and repeat for the next room.
  6. Open the work order preview and confirm the room, cut height, and selected wall information are present.

Other wall work

  • Carbon straps: use automatic spacing when appropriate; use a custom count only after field verification.
  • Insulation: distinguish removal from new/blown insulation and set the affected room or wall scope.
  • Foam and bond pockets: enter room-specific quantity and location notes.
  • Structural work: place beams/posts on the drawing and add installation notes for the crew.
Do not rely on notes alone. Select the affected room and wall whenever the tool supports it; then use notes for exceptions and field details.

9. Review materials, labor, rentals, and pricing

  1. Finish the room and scope review before pricing. A changed measurement can change quantities.
  2. Open Materials and Internal Costing.
  3. Review automatic materials against the actual scope. Correct the room/work selection that produced a wrong quantity.
  4. Select Margin or Markup according to company rules, then set the target value.
  5. Add labor using the company’s project, detailed, or combined method.
  6. Add rentals such as dehumidifiers, air movers, scrubbers, and any approved delivery/handling charges.
  7. Use catalog items where possible. Add a custom item with a clear customer description when no catalog item fits.
  8. Set tax and verify the subtotal, tax, deposit, and total.
Private screen: internal cost, margin, and markup may be restricted by permission and should not be shown in customer-facing mode.

10. Build the proposal, invoice, and work order

All three documents begin with one shared scope table. Review it before generating anything.

Review the shared document scope

  1. Tap Documents.
  2. Read every scope description and confirm its quantity and customer price.
  3. Use the Proposal/Invoice checkbox for customer-facing rows.
  4. Use the Work Order checkbox for rows the crew must perform or know about.
  5. Add a custom scope item only once; then choose which documents include it.
  6. Remove duplicate or internal-only wording from customer rows.

Customer proposal

  1. Enter/confirm proposal number, customer details, and the selected scope.
  2. Choose whether to display blueprint, detailed pricing, totals, and terms.
  3. Preview the proposal and check page breaks, customer spelling, scope, exclusions, total, and payment terms.
  4. Use the browser print dialog to print or Save as PDF.

Invoice

  1. Enter invoice number, due date, deposit/payment information, and payment terms.
  2. Set status to Draft, Sent, Partially paid, or Paid as the invoice progresses.
  3. Confirm that only billable customer rows are selected.
  4. Preview, then print or save as PDF. Reopen the saved job after payment updates and change the status.

Crew work order

  1. Open Work Order and select the assigned crew.
  2. Select the rooms and tasks included in this visit.
  3. Add instructions for access, protection, sequencing, disposal, equipment, and customer restrictions.
  4. Confirm that room dimensions, wall-specific cuts, equipment, and selected scope are visible.
  5. Choose Generate & Print and save or distribute the packet according to company policy.
Consistency check: proposal and invoice should agree on customer scope and price; the work order can include extra non-priced crew instructions.

11. Save, export, recover, or remove a job

Normal saving

  • RoomFlow autosaves working data locally and syncs cloud jobs when you are signed in and connected. Wait for Cloud Online before closing the source device.
  • On another PC or mobile device, sign in to the same company and choose Jobs â†’ Refresh Shared. Cloud jobs restore the layout; permitted financial users also receive protected costing, and saved estimate drafts restore their lines.
  • Save Project downloads a roomflow_project.json backup.
  • Load Project imports a previously exported RoomFlow JSON file.
  • Before closing a device in weak service, export a JSON backup and confirm the job card exists.

Remove a job from the RoomFlow Jobs dashboard

  1. Open Jobs and find the correct card.
  2. Use its delete/remove action and confirm.
  3. The dashboard sends the job to More → Trash & Recovery when recovery is supported.
  4. Use Undo immediately or open Trash & Recovery to restore it.
Dashboard removal and Tracker deletion are different. Dashboard trash is recoverable. Tracker deletion removes the shared cloud job permanently after permission, safety, and typed-confirmation checks.

12. Use RoomFlow Tracker

Tracker uses the same RoomFlow sign-in and company. It is the office/TV view for jobs, shared equipment, drop-offs, and intake health.

Jobs

  1. Open Tracker, sign in, and choose the correct company.
  2. Search by customer, address, or phone, or filter by job status.
  3. Use Add Job for a manual job, or edit an existing card to update contact, issue, appointment, notes, or status.
  4. Use Refresh to request the newest cloud data. The board may rotate pages automatically; pause rotation when editing or reading.
  5. Watch the Zapier intake health strip for the latest delivery status.

Permanently delete a Tracker job

  1. Open the job card and choose Delete.
  2. Read the customer/job name in the confirmation screen.
  3. If equipment is actively dropped off to that job, pick it up or reassign it first. Tracker blocks deletion to protect inventory records.
  4. Type DELETE exactly and confirm permanent deletion.
  5. If Delete is unavailable, an administrator must grant your role the delete_jobs capability.
Permanent means permanent: Tracker cloud deletion does not move the job to RoomFlow Trash. Export anything you must retain before confirming.

Equipment Inventory

  1. Open Equipment and choose Add Equipment Type.
  2. Enter a clear equipment name and total owned quantity.
  3. Edit the item when inventory changes. Tracker calculates deployed and available counts from active drop-offs.

Drop-offs

  1. Open Drop-offs and choose New Drop-off.
  2. Select the job, equipment/items, quantity, drop-off time, expected pickup, location, and notes.
  3. Confirm the drop-off; available inventory updates.
  4. When equipment returns, open the deployment and mark it picked up. Verify available quantity increased.

13. Connect Gmail/OutreachGenius through Zapier

The Zap watches caller emails, sends the original email data to RoomFlow’s protected intake endpoint, and RoomFlow parses it into a job/lead.

Security first: a webhook secret is a password. If it was pasted in chat, email, screenshots, or another public location, rotate it in RoomFlow/Supabase and replace it in Zapier. Never place it in the JSON body.

Before building the Zap

  1. Have an administrator deploy the current Supabase intake-lead Edge Function and database migrations.
  2. In RoomFlow, open Settings → Email Intake / Zapier.
  3. Create or rotate the endpoint secret and copy the full POST URL and secret immediately.
  4. Store the secret in Zapier’s request header. RoomFlow cannot display the secret again.

Build the Zap

  1. Trigger app: Gmail. Event: New Email Matching Search.
  2. Set a narrow Gmail search for the caller-notification sender/subject so unrelated email is never sent.
  3. Test the trigger with a real OutreachGenius/caller email.
  4. Action app: Webhooks by Zapier. Event: POST.
  5. Paste the RoomFlow POST URL.
  6. Set payload type to JSON and send Gmail’s stable message ID, sender, subject, body plain, and body HTML. Include the lead source when available.
  7. Add request header x-roomflow-webhook-secret with the secret and Content-Type: application/json.
  8. Test the action. A successful result reports ok: true, a job ID, normalized fields, and any parsing warnings.
  9. Publish the Zap, send one new test caller email, and verify it in both RoomFlow inbound leads and Tracker.

Recommended JSON mapping

{
  "source_message_id": "{{Gmail Message ID}}",
  "source_sender": "{{From Email}}",
  "source_subject": "{{Subject}}",
  "body_plain": "{{Body Plain}}",
  "body_html": "{{Body HTML}}",
  "lead_source": "OutreachGenius"
}
The repository includes ZAPIER_COPILOT_OUTREACHGENIUS.md, which can be supplied to Zapier Copilot as build instructions, plus ZAPIER_SETUP.md for deployment and error details.

Verify and diagnose

  1. RoomFlow: open Email Intake settings and choose Refresh Activity.
  2. Tracker: inspect the Zapier intake health strip.
  3. If a lead is missing, open Zapier Task History and inspect the HTTP status/response.
  4. 401/403: replace the URL/secret, confirm the header name, and check endpoint permissions.
  5. 400/422: map subject and original body fields; do not send only a formatted summary.
  6. Duplicate jobs: map Gmail’s stable Message ID so retries update the same intake record.

15. Create or update a Townsquare draft

Draft only: RoomFlow never sends, issues, approves, emails, accepts, or charges a Townsquare estimate. Review and send it manually inside Townsquare.

One-time company setup

  1. An integration manager opens More → Townsquare Interactive.
  2. Turn on the integration and choose Auto or Browser Bridge. Current official API documentation does not include a separate service-property endpoint, so Auto uses Browser Bridge for the complete workflow.
  3. Enter the HTTPS Townsquare business-management destination and save.
  4. On desktop Chrome or Edge, install the unpacked extension from townsquare-bridge-extension.
  5. Open the extension popup, save the same Townsquare URL, and grant access to that site.
  6. Sign in to Townsquare normally. The extension does not read your password or export cookies.
  7. Choose Start Guided Mapping. Click each requested Townsquare control, then finish the mapping.

Create the first draft

  1. Finish the RoomFlow customer and complete service address.
  2. Add at least one estimate item. Verify quantity, unit price, discount, tax, and total.
  3. Save the RoomFlow draft.
  4. Press Create Townsquare Draft.
  5. Watch the progress dialog. If several customers or properties match, select the correct record; RoomFlow never merges them automatically.
  6. Townsquare opens in the authenticated desktop browser. Do not close the tab while the bridge is working.
  7. RoomFlow shows Draft Created only after Townsquare confirms a draft ID, DRAFT status, and the same total.
  8. Choose Review in Townsquare. Make the final review and send manually when ready.

Update an existing draft

  1. Change and save the RoomFlow estimate.
  2. Press Update Townsquare Draft.
  3. RoomFlow uses the saved Townsquare estimate ID. If the mapped draft cannot be found, the workflow stops instead of creating a duplicate.
  4. If Townsquare says the estimate is issued, accepted, rejected, paid, cancelled, expired, or otherwise finalized, RoomFlow will not overwrite it.

Android

Desktop extensions do not run inside Android apps or normal Android WebViews. When an always-on Sync Station is paired, Android queues the Townsquare draft directly to that station. Without a paired station, open the same estimate in desktop RoomFlow with Chrome or Edge and run the draft action there.

If Townsquare changes its page, open RoomFlow Settings and choose Test / Reset Mapping. Only selector settings are saved; customer and estimate payloads remain temporary.

16. Troubleshooting

I added an item, but it is missing from a proposal, invoice, or work order.
  1. Open Documents and find the row in the shared scope.
  2. Check Proposal/Invoice and/or Work Order for that row.
  3. Confirm quantity is above zero and customer price is set when needed.
  4. Regenerate the document; do not rely on an older preview/PDF.
I cannot delete a job in Tracker.
  1. Confirm the current deployed version includes permanent job deletion.
  2. Make sure your role has delete_jobs.
  3. Pick up/reassign any active equipment deployment for the job.
  4. Type DELETE exactly in the confirmation.
  5. If it still fails, capture the displayed error and ask an administrator to review Supabase permissions/logs.
An angled room starts or appears in the wrong place.
  1. Use the current RoomFlow version, select Draw Angled Room, and tap the intended first point.
  2. Finish the polygon, select it, and drag the whole room or individual corners.
  3. If point order is wrong or lines cross, use Undo and redraw the outline in order.
A Zapier caller email did not arrive.
  1. Refresh RoomFlow intake activity and Tracker.
  2. Check Zapier Task History and read the response.
  3. Confirm the Gmail trigger matched the email and the action sent the original subject/body.
  4. For 401/403, update the secret header. For parsing warnings, add body plain/body HTML.
A job is missing or seems out of date.
  1. Confirm that both devices use a RoomFlow sign-in and the exact same active company.
  2. On the source device, reconnect and wait for Cloud Online.
  3. On the receiving device, open Jobs, choose Refresh Shared, and remove search filters.
  4. Look for the Cloud Shared badge. A Local badge means that copy has not completed cloud sync.
  5. Check More → Trash & Recovery.
  6. If offline, reconnect and allow sync to finish. Use Load Project if you exported a JSON backup.
A Townsquare draft did not complete.
  1. Confirm the desktop extension is installed and its popup shows the correct Townsquare destination.
  2. Sign in to Townsquare normally, then retry from RoomFlow.
  3. If a required control is missing, run Test / Reset Mapping and map the named control.
  4. If totals differ, compare tax, discount, quantities, and prices in both systems. RoomFlow stops before claiming success.
  5. If the existing estimate is finalized, review it in Townsquare; RoomFlow will not overwrite it.
  6. An integration manager can open View Diagnostics. Logs are sanitized and do not contain credentials or cookies.
Camera/AR/LiDAR scanning is unavailable.
  1. Open More → Diagnostics and check device/browser support and permission.
  2. Allow camera access in browser/device settings and use a secure HTTPS site.
  3. Reload RoomFlow. If scanning remains unavailable, use manual dimensions or CAD drawing; job data is not lost.

17. Final quality-control checklist

  • Correct customer, address, phone, email, estimator, and company.
  • Every work level and room is present with verified dimensions.
  • Odd-shaped rooms do not overlap and are attached correctly.
  • Doors, windows, equipment, and structural items are placed correctly.
  • Drywall and other wall work identifies the exact room, walls, and cut height.
  • Materials, labor, rentals, custom items, margin/markup, and tax are reviewed.
  • The shared Documents scope has correct quantities, prices, and inclusion checkboxes.
  • Proposal and invoice customer scope/pricing agree.
  • If Townsquare is used, the draft status and total are confirmed and the final send remains manual.
  • Work order contains crew, rooms, dimensions, wall work, equipment, and access notes.
  • Job is saved, cloud sync is confirmed, and an export/PDF backup exists when required.

Done: once every item above is checked, the estimate is ready for customer and crew use.
