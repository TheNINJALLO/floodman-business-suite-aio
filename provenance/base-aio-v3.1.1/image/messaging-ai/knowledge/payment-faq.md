# Floodman approved account FAQ

- Floodman invoices are due upon receipt when sent.
- Secure online payments are processed by Square. Floodman customer-message systems do not store card numbers or card security codes.
- The customer portal displays the current recorded balance, signed documents, invoice links, and payment links.
- A customer who believes a balance is incorrect, work is incomplete, property was damaged, insurance should pay, or a refund is due must be routed to a Floodman employee. Automated reminders must pause.
- A customer may request a copy of an invoice, receipt, payment link, or staff callback.
- A promise to pay does not alter the original invoice terms. It only pauses automated reminders through the recorded date when allowed by Floodman policy.
